## Method

1. Every PDF was opened with PyMuPDF. None contain a text layer (all text is printed inside the images), and each page holds exactly one embedded JPEG — the product plate. That original JPEG was extracted at full resolution (no page re-rendering).
2. Product names, numbers and colourways were transcribed from the labels printed on each plate (verified from enlarged label crops).
3. Printed banners, name tags and 'FRONT VIEW / BACK VIEW' captions were removed by cropping. Where a plate shows a front and a back view, the split point is found automatically (the emptiest column between the two garments) and each view is trimmed to its content. Images were never retouched, recoloured or generated.
4. Each product folder holds: `-01` front (or main) view, `-02` back view, `-03` front-and-back view and, for multi-purpose aprons, `-04` detail callouts printed in the catalogue.
5. Contact sheets of every output image are in `reports/contact-sheets/` for quick review.

## Source quality notes

- EPS Series A pages (1–10) are low resolution (768 px wide plates); the resulting images are small.
- Many plates carry Everbright watermarks across the background; these are part of the original photographs and were kept.
- No product descriptions, sizes or available-colour lists are printed in the PDFs, so those fields are empty. Only printed facts are shown (e.g. 'Water proof material', multi-purpose apron detail callouts, 'Reversible — same design on both sides', 'Previous design').
